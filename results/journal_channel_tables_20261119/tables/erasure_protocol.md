# Independent coordinate erasure at p=0.50

The channel sends b ⊙ f, where each coordinate of b is an independent Bernoulli(0.50) keep indicator. The matched bank contains m iid complete mask vectors and is reused during training. Deployment draws fresh masks. Perfect training uses the identity channel. This erasure study uses one fixed erasure probability throughout.

The principal bank sizes are m=200,500,1000 for two moons, MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10. The references are m=2000 for those four datasets and m=100 for CIFAR-10. The source configurations are the seven distinct, validation-selected objectives from the previous all-Bayesian m=20 erasure study. The original matched baselines are ERM and expected-squared-norm L2; T1 and T2 denote the separated and joint training objectives. The perfect-training versions of T1 and T2 have identical hyperparameters and objectives at the source and share one row in the new table.

The initial comparison keeps the optimizer, model, sample size, epochs, posterior initialization and five paired seeds 73101–73105 from the original erasure comparison. It uses the fading study's data split seed 20261118 and 20 posterior/deployment draws per seed. The frozen dimensionless regularization strength is held fixed as m changes: T1 keeps its channel coefficient and scales its data coefficient by sqrt((20-1)/(m-1)); T2 rescales the joint penalty coefficient by sqrt((1+19/(n-1))/(1+(m-1)/(n-1))). Perfect, ERM and L2 training hyperparameters are shared across m apart from bank size. The repository saves every transferred configuration, source identity and seed.

When a subsequent search is used, each method receives comparable validation tuning and the selected configurations are evaluated on fresh paired confirmation seeds. All primary and follow-up reports remain accessible. Journal cells report the arithmetic mean of the per-training-seed test accuracies ± their sample standard deviation, both in percentage units. The raw means determine bolding of displayed winners.
